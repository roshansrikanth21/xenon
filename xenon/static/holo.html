Xenon Holo · Guide

Your hands are the controller.

01 · SET UP

CAMERAAllow the camera. The video is processed in this tab and never uploaded.
LIGHTFace a lamp or window. Tracking struggles in the dark or with a bright window behind you.
DISTANCESit back about an arm's length so both hands fit, palms toward the camera.

02 · GESTURES

MENUPinch and hold in empty space until the ring fills, or double-pinch / double-click. Pinch a button to pick it.
PICK / PRESSA quick pinch (thumb and index tap) on a button, tile, part or shard.
POINTIndex out, other fingers curled. Highlights what you point at; your fingertip glows gold.
MOVEMake a fist on the object and move your hand. Turn your wrist to rotate it; push toward the camera to bring it closer.
SPINPoint and flick your finger in any direction. It coasts to a stop.
RESIZEPinch with both hands, then pull apart or push together.
CLAPTwo open hands meeting quickly. What it does depends on the mode.

03 · MODES

XENONYour music, top artists, CPU and RAM, modes and Talk on panels round the core. Pinch to press, fist-drag to spin the ring, hold an open palm and Xenon listens.
ARC REACTORExplode: two fists pulled apart, palms together then apart fast, or twist a fist counter-clockwise. Exploded: pinch a part to pull it out, pinch and hold for its spec card; drop it near its slot and it snaps home. Assemble: clap or twist a fist clockwise. Key E.
PERIODIC LABPoint to browse the elements; a quick pinch adds an atom to the mix (up to 12). Pinch a chip to remove it. Clap or pinch MIX: real molecules (H₂O, CO₂, CH₄, NaCl…) show their structure, anything else becomes a new element. Clap with an empty tray to bring the table back.
SHARDSSix shards of Xenon: Music, Memory, Voice, Vision, Time, Power. Pinch one to take it out and read its file (live Xenon data). Flip an open hand palm-up for its artifact, palm-down for the shard. Clap to pull all six into the Xenon core; clap again to release them.

04 · SETTINGS & KEYS

QUALITYAUTO picks for this laptop. HIGH sharpest, MEDIUM most laptops, LOW lighter, SAFE runs tracking on the CPU. Or add ?quality=low to the link.
VIEWMIRROR flips the view, DEPTH INVERT swaps push/pull, B&W the holographic grey look, DIM darkens a bright room, CAMERA switches cameras.
KEYSM menu · E explode/assemble · F mirror · I depth invert · B black & white · C next camera · L labels · O hands overlay · D debug · Q quality · H guide · Esc close
